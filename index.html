Top LC

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Top LC is the student app of Top Learning Center, a language center in Urgench, Uzbekistan. The app is only for the center's students and their parents. Accounts are created by the center; you cannot sign up in the app.

1. What we collect

  • Account details the center enters when you enrol: name, login ID, phone number, group, branch and course.
  • Study records: timetable, attendance, marks, homework, ranking points and payments for your courses.
  • Location, only when you check in. When you scan the classroom QR code, the app reads your location once to confirm you are at the center. It is not tracked in the background.
  • Camera, only to scan the check-in QR code. No photos or video are saved.
  • Profile photo, if you choose one from your photo library.
  • Messages you send in class chats and to teachers.
  • Device token for push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging), so the app can tell you about lessons, marks and announcements.

2. How we use it

Only to run your studies at Top Learning Center: show your timetable and results, record attendance, send notifications, and let you talk with your teachers and group. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use it for tracking across other apps and websites.

3. Who can see it

  • Staff of Top Learning Center (teachers and administrators), each seeing only what their role needs.
  • Members of your class chats see the messages you send there.
  • Google Firebase delivers push notifications on our behalf.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored on the center's servers and sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Your sign-in token is kept in the phone's secure storage (Keychain on iPhone).

5. How long we keep it

While you study at the center and as long as the center needs your records afterwards (for example, certificates and payment history). Ask the center to delete your account and data at any time.

6. Children

Some students are under 18. Their accounts are created by the center with the consent of a parent or guardian, who can ask the center to view, correct or delete the child's data.

7. Your choices

  • You can turn off notifications, camera and location in your phone's settings. Without location and camera you cannot check in by QR, but everything else still works.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data.

8. Contact
